## Tournament Special Rule
### "Throw Anyone"
If a player on your team has the ***Throw Team-mate*** skill, they may throw any oppostion player with the ***Right Stuff*** skill. Player with ***Throw Team-mate*** must be adjacent to a player with ***Right Stuff*** when performing the action.

As per Blood Bowl 2025 ruleset, you may only declare ***Throw Team-mate*** once per turn regardless of how many players on your team have the skill.

#### Performing ***Throw Anyone***
1. Declare ***Throw Anyone*** and select the player attempting ***Throw Anyone*** and the player with ***Right Stuff*** attempting to be thrown.
2. Roll for any traits for player with ***Throw Team-mate*** attempting ***Throw Anyone*** \(eg. Bonehead, Really Stupid, Take Root etc\).
3. Coach of ***Right Stuff*** player rolls for "Squirm Free".
    - Roll a D6, and on a 2+ the player "Squirms Free". If the result is a 1, continue ***Throw Anyone*** with standard the ***Throw Team-mate*** procedure for the player who declared ***Thorw Anyone***.
    - If the player does "Squirms Free", the player is dropped and will bounce from the throwing players square \(single D8 scatter\) and must attempt to land. 
    - Make an Agility roll for a safe landing. This roll is unmodified unless landing into an oppositions tackle zone. Apply a -1 modifier for each opposition player marking the square the thrown player will land in, not including the player attempting to ***Throw Anyone***.
    - If the player with ***Right Stuff*** lands in a occupied square, follow the ***Landing in an Occupied Square*** rules.
    - If the player with ***Right Stuff*** is Knocked Down due to bouncing into an occupied square and is the ball carrier, the ball will Bounce from the unoccupied square they eventually land in.
    - Attempting ***Throw Anyone*** player's activation ends.
4. If ***Right Stuff*** player does not "Squirm free" continue Throw Team-mate procedure as normal. ***Always Hungry*** does not take effect on opposition players.
